## Summary (from fetch)

The LLM Wiki pattern: instead of dumping documents into RAG and re-deriving knowledge on every query, an LLM agent incrementally maintains a persistent wiki of Markdown files.

**Core metaphor:** "Obsidian is the IDE; the LLM is the programmer; the wiki is the codebase."

## Three core operations

1. **Ingest** — New source → LLM reads, writes summary, updates index/entity pages, appends to log.
2. **Query** — User asks question → LLM searches index, reads relevant pages, synthesizes answer.
3. **Lint** — Periodic health check for contradictions, orphaned pages, missing cross-references.

## Mandatory files

- `index.md` — Catalog of all wiki pages with one-line summaries, organized by category. Updated on every ingest operation.
- `log.md` — Append-only chronological record of ingest/query/lint actions. Entries follow format `## [YYYY-MM-DD] action | title` for unix parsing.
- A **schema document** (e.g. `CLAUDE.md`) — Configuration file defining wiki structure, conventions, LLM workflows.

## Authorial philosophy (quoted from the gist via fetch)

> "The tedious part of maintaining a knowledge base is not the reading or the thinking — it's the bookkeeping. Humans abandon wikis because maintenance burden grows faster than value. LLMs don't get bored, don't forget to update a cross-reference."

The pattern treats the LLM as a **disciplined wiki maintainer**, not a chatbot. Without a detailed schema, the agent "resets to zero" each session. With one, it inherits full operational context.

**Roles:**
- Human: curate sources, direct analysis, decide what to explore.
- LLM: summarize, extract, cross-reference, maintain consistency.
