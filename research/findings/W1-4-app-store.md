# W1-4 — Autodesk App Store mechanics + monetization + Design and Make Marketplace certification

**Date:** 2026-06-11
**Researcher:** workflow sub-agent (Stage 1 Wave 1)

## Summary (3-5 sentences)

The Autodesk App Store has been absorbed into the **Design and Make Marketplace** (marketplace.autodesk.com) — one storefront, one Publisher Center, one submission pipeline covering desktop apps, web apps, and now MCP servers; legacy apps.autodesk.com pages carry the Marketplace branding and old detail URLs 404. Monetization is unusually favorable: Autodesk currently takes **zero commission** (the Publisher Agreement reserves the right to take up to 30% in future), with payments flowing directly to the publisher via PayPal/BlueSnap. The MCP publishing path is **open to new entrants** — primary Autodesk text says "the marketplace is open to every developer, MCP author, APS builder" — and at least two third-party MCP connectors (both Revit-facing) are already live. The certification gate is a manifest + security-attestation review, not a paid program; the "ISO 42001-aligned" framing applies to Autodesk's own certification, and is only community-attributed for third-party review. The weakest link in the chain is update delivery: there is no auto-update or in-product update notification for store-installed add-ins.

## First principles (what the thing IS, stripped of marketing)

- The Design and Make Marketplace is a **curated catalog + payment pass-through + installer-packaging service**. Autodesk reviews, hosts, and builds your installer; it does not (currently) take money or push updates.
- MCP certification is, mechanically, **a JSON manifest plus a signed security questionnaire emailed to a review inbox**. It is a trust/disclosure gate, not a technical conformance test suite.
- The Marketplace's strategic function is to become the **registry that Autodesk Assistant reads from**: certified MCPs become tools an in-product agent can invoke. Listing = human discoverability today, agent discoverability later.
- The legacy App Store machinery (Publisher Corner, appsubmissions@autodesk.com, PayPal/BlueSnap rails, Autodesk-built installers) survives underneath the new brand.

## Findings

### 1. App Store submission process end-to-end

Process (desktop app): sign in with a free Autodesk SSO account → Publisher Corner → accept the Publisher Agreement → "Publish a New Product" → select app type (desktop/web) and OS → upload files and assets (description, screenshots, video, help-page content) → submit ([Desktop App Submission Process Overview PDF](https://damassets.autodesk.net/content/dam/autodesk/www/adn/pdf/desktop-app-submission-process-overview.pdf) — fetched 2026-06-11). The Marketplace getting-started guide adds: "The Marketplace team reviews your submission for suitability. We'll email you with feedback or next steps. If accepted, Autodesk creates the final package file for you to review" ([Marketplace getting started](https://aps.autodesk.com/marketplace/getting-started) — fetched 2026-06-11).

Timeline: initial contact "within 24hr to 48hr of submitting," then "2 to 3 working days to do in-depth testing of the app" ([Publisher FAQ](https://damassets.autodesk.net/content/dam/autodesk/www/adn/pdf/frequently-asked-questions.pdf), via search snippets — direct PDF extraction returned font data; timeline figures are from search-indexed FAQ text).

Rejection criteria (non-exhaustive, from [Product Guidelines](https://apps.autodesk.com/Publisher/ProductGuidelines) — fetched 2026-06-11): product crashes or significantly slows the Autodesk product; harmful material; gathering confidential data without permission; certain open-source licensing; misuse of customer data; attempts to discover passwords; trademark/copyright misuse or implying Autodesk endorsement; illegal file sharing; gambling; false/misleading/defamatory content. Autodesk "reserve[s] the right to pull the Product out of the Autodesk App Store without advance notice" post-approval.

### 2. Monetization and Autodesk's cut

Price types in live use: Free, Trial, Free trial, Paid (e.g. "$5"), and Subscription [empirical — observed on store search listings, fetched 2026-06-11]. Payments: "The Autodesk App Store uses PayPal™ and BlueSnap® as its payment vendors"; subscription publishers "receive notification and payment data directly from PayPal and BlueSnap" ([Publisher FAQ](https://damassets.autodesk.net/content/dam/autodesk/www/adn/pdf/frequently-asked-questions.pdf) — fetched 2026-06-11).

The cut: "At some point in the future Autodesk may take a commission that could be up to 30% — but for now Autodesk is taking zero commission on the sales of your products using the Autodesk App Store" (Publisher FAQ, referencing [Publisher Agreement](https://apps.autodesk.com/Content/pdf/Publisher.pdf) sections 1.8 and 4.3 — fetched 2026-06-11). A publisher PayPal account is required "if charging" ([Marketplace getting started](https://aps.autodesk.com/marketplace/getting-started)).

### 3. Listing requirements: code signing, security, privacy, support

- **Privacy policy:** a "Publisher privacy policy" is a listed requirement for all apps ([Marketplace getting started](https://aps.autodesk.com/marketplace/getting-started) — fetched 2026-06-11). Live listings expose "Publisher Privacy Policy" and "End User Agreement (EUA)" links plus Support and FAQ tabs [empirical — AUTOM8LABS listing, fetched 2026-06-11].
- **Code signing:** the [Fusion publishing guidelines](https://aps.autodesk.com/marketplace/publisher-center/fusion-publisher-guidelines) (fetched 2026-06-11) contain **no publisher-side code-signing requirement**. Autodesk builds the installer: "We strongly recommend you make use of the standard the Autodesk App Store app installer we create for you. Installers can be created for Windows and Mac." Whether Autodesk signs all generated installers is [unverified]; community threads report inconsistent signatures on Fusion store installers ([forum thread](https://forums.autodesk.com/t5/fusion-manage-ideas/no-digital-signature-for-some-f360-apps-from-app-store/idi-p/10290173), via search — community source).
- **Fusion technical bar:** add-in must ship as a `.bundle` folder with `PackageContents.xml`; "Keep your load time under 0.005 seconds" (measured via `APPLOG_FOR_PERFORMANCE`); must be "relevant to (and usable with) the latest version of Fusion" (Fusion publishing guidelines).
- **MCP-specific security review:** MCP Tool Manifest (JSON declaring all tools, resources, prompts, external endpoints, Autodesk APIs, AI/LLM providers) + Publisher Declaration Form with security attestations; "All external connections must use HTTPS"; "Only access the minimum data required for functionality"; if data goes to AI services you must "declare the provider … describe what data is sent and obtain user consent" ([MCP publisher guide](https://aps.autodesk.com/marketplace/mcp-publisher-guide) — fetched 2026-06-11).

### 4. Design and Make Marketplace vs App Store

They are **the same thing now — a rebrand-plus-expansion, not parallel pipelines**. Evidence: appstore.autodesk.com and aps.autodesk.com/design-and-make-marketplace both resolve/301 to marketplace.autodesk.com [empirical — redirect observed 2026-06-11]; apps.autodesk.com pages now render under the title "Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace" [empirical]; legacy App Store detail URLs (e.g. the old Fusion MCP listing id) now 404 with a Marketplace-branded error page [empirical]. The APS blog frames it as "a new destination for developers to publish applications, integrations, and AI-powered solutions" ([Building for Agentic AI](https://aps.autodesk.com/blog/building-agentic-ai-whats-new-autodesk-platform-services) — fetched 2026-06-11).

One submission pipeline: the [Marketplace getting started](https://aps.autodesk.com/marketplace/getting-started) guide covers desktop apps, web apps, **and MCP servers** in a single flow, with MCP servers adding two artifacts (Tool Manifest + Publisher Declaration). **An MCP goes to the Marketplace — there is no separate "App Store" destination anymore.** Certified MCPs additionally "become eligible to be called by Autodesk Assistant" — a distribution channel ordinary add-ins don't get ([Marketplace blog](https://aps.autodesk.com/blog/design-and-make-marketplace-where-your-solutions-meet-industry-agentic-ai-workflows) — fetched 2026-06-11).

### 5. Is MCP Publisher certification open? Approved third-party examples?

**Open — the known gap is settled.** Primary source: "The marketplace is open to every developer, MCP author, APS builder, and problem solver working in Design & Make" ([Marketplace blog](https://aps.autodesk.com/blog/design-and-make-marketplace-where-your-solutions-meet-industry-agentic-ai-workflows) — fetched 2026-06-11). It is curated, not gatekept by invitation: "The marketplace isn't an open directory. Before a solution can be listed, it goes through Autodesk's quality and standards review" (same source). Submission is by email (appsubmissions@autodesk.com) with explicit resubmission-after-rejection guidance — no fees mentioned ([MCP publisher guide](https://aps.autodesk.com/marketplace/mcp-publisher-guide)).

Published third-party examples (both Revit-facing): **AUTOM8LABS MCP Connector** — free, 36 query tools, "connects Claude Desktop and Cursor directly to your Autodesk® Revit® session" ([listing](https://marketplace.autodesk.com/apps/0639863e-7269-49a3-aade-a49442c010d4) — fetched 2026-06-11); **NonicaTab FREE — Revit Tools + AI Connector MCP** by Nonica [empirical — store search result, fetched 2026-06-11]. No third-party MCP for **Fusion** was found. On ISO 42001: **Autodesk itself** achieved ISO 42001 certification for its AI Management System ([AU 2025 takeaways, Autodesk News](https://adsknews.autodesk.com/en/news/the-3-biggest-autodesk-ai-takeaways-from-au-2025) — fetched 2026-06-11); the claim that *third-party* MCP certification is "ISO 42001-aligned" appears only in community analysis (FifthRow, per P1) and **not** in the primary publisher guide — downgrade to [unverified].

### 6. Autodesk developer program

ADN membership pricing: **1 user $1,750; 2–5 users $3,000; 6+ users $5,500** (USD in Americas; currency varies by region), per calendar year, not prorated ([ADN membership](https://aps.autodesk.com/developer/overview/autodesk-developer-network-membership) — fetched 2026-06-11). It unlocks "access to virtually all Autodesk software for development, support, and field demonstration purposes," pre-release betas, and "one-on-one technical support from an Autodesk software engineer." **Free paths:** startups qualify "at no charge for up to 3 years"; university developers free; Oct–Dec joiners get the following year included. ADN membership is **not listed as a requirement** to publish — the Marketplace requirements checklist is only: product-guideline review, privacy policy, PayPal account if charging ([Marketplace getting started](https://aps.autodesk.com/marketplace/getting-started)); ADN is positioned as an optional resource. [unverified: no page found that states "ADN not required" in those words — inferred from the requirements checklist.]

### 7. Update/versioning mechanics

Per the Publisher FAQ + Fusion guidelines: every resubmission "must have an updated version number"; description-only edits are "Auto Published without review," while changes to the uploaded file, price type (e.g. trial/free → paid), or product-version compatibility trigger re-review; "your existing version will remain in the store until it is reviewed and replaced" ([Publisher FAQ](https://damassets.autodesk.net/content/dam/autodesk/www/adn/pdf/frequently-asked-questions.pdf), via search; [Fusion publishing guidelines](https://aps.autodesk.com/marketplace/publisher-center/fusion-publisher-guidelines) — fetched 2026-06-11).

**Reaching users is manual.** There is no auto-update or in-product notification for store-installed add-ins; the documented mechanism is the publisher exporting download records ("Export Download Record," available 48 hours after download, in Publisher Corner) and emailing customers ([Publisher FAQ](https://damassets.autodesk.net/content/dam/autodesk/www/adn/pdf/frequently-asked-questions.pdf); [Fusion forum feature request](https://forums.autodesk.com/t5/fusion-api-and-scripts-forum/feature-request-notifying-users-about-available-updates-of/td-p/6540464), via search — community source). No 2026 documentation contradicting this was found.

## Decision implications for adze-fusion

- **Distribution economics are a non-issue:** zero commission today, direct PayPal/BlueSnap payments, free submission, Autodesk-built Win+Mac installers. The 30% reserve clause is the only pricing risk to model.
- **The MCP certification door is open and nearly empty for Fusion.** Third-party MCPs are being approved (Revit examples live), but no third-party Fusion MCP is listed — a first-mover certification slot exists, and certified MCPs are the only third-party class slated for Autodesk Assistant invocation.
- **The manifest/declaration regime constrains architecture:** every tool, external endpoint, and AI provider must be declared; data sent to AI services requires disclosed scope and user consent. Multi-provider designs must be declared per-provider.
- **No auto-update channel** means a published add-in needs its own update-check mechanism or accepts stale installs.
- **Subscription pricing is supported natively** — viable for a SaaS-shaped adze without building payment rails.
